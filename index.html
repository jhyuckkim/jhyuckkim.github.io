About

I research and train large language models at KRAFTON AI, led by Kangwook Lee, and collaborate closely with Dimitris Papailiopoulos. Recently, I have been working on both model architectures for pre-training and reinforcement learning for post-training (yes, we are a small team!).

I am particularly interested in efficiency, with the long-term goal of building AI systems that are useful in practice, even under tight resource constraints. To this end, I work on reducing the memory footprint of inference and reasoning, as well as the computational cost of training. More recently, I have been exploring efficient agentic systems for automated research.

Previously, I completed my master’s at Cambridge, where I worked in the Machine Learning Group under the supervision of Richard Turner.
